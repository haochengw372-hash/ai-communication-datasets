# Data use and redistribution

The code is Apache-2.0. Original explanatory documentation and derived aggregate
tables may be reused under CC BY 4.0, with attribution to Haocheng Wang and this
version. Neither license grants rights in third-party X content. Post identifiers
are shared as research references, with no claim that the authors can relicense
underlying posts, images, user profiles, or platform rights.

X's [current Developer Policy](https://docs.x.com/developer-terms/policy) restricts
content redistribution and includes rules on IDs, research uses, access, and
content changes. Review applicable terms for your own collection and use. This
repository makes no legal determination that a use is compliant, and an ID-only
release is not a substitute for applicable permissions or institutional review.

Use your own authorized access to retrieve currently available content. Respect
unavailable/protected content and deletion or removal requests. Do not attempt
to identify protected characteristics, match ordinary users to private identities,
or use this research corpus for targeting or harassment. Do not redistribute
hydrated raw content merely because the collection scripts are open source.

Send correction/removal requests via a GitHub issue containing the ID and request,
without reposting sensitive content. IDs are linkable and are not anonymous.
